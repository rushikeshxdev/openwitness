# 📜 The Constitution of OpenWitness

> "Every claim deserves evidence.
> Every piece of evidence deserves verification."

---

# Preamble

OpenWitness is an open-source platform for preserving, organizing, and verifying evidence from public events.

Our mission is not to decide what is true.

Our mission is to help people understand **why something should be trusted** through transparent evidence, independent verification, and open collaboration.

OpenWitness exists to preserve history—not rewrite it.

---

# Mission

Build the world's most transparent evidence platform.

Not a news platform.

Not a social network.

Not a government portal.

An Evidence Intelligence Platform.

---

# Vision

A future where every public event can be explored through structured evidence instead of opinions.

---

# Core Philosophy

Truth is rarely established by a single source.

Trust emerges from multiple independent pieces of evidence.

OpenWitness measures confidence—not absolute truth.

---

# Principle 1

## Evidence over Opinion

Opinions are welcome in discussions.

Evidence powers decisions.

Every important claim should be backed by evidence.

---

# Principle 2

## Trust Nobody Blindly

Do not blindly trust:

- AI
- Journalists
- Governments
- Organizations
- Community Members
- Moderators

Every participant contributes signals.

Nobody contributes absolute truth.

---

# Principle 3

## Verification Must Be Explainable

Never display:

Verified

without explaining

WHY

Every verification should include:

✓ Metadata matched

✓ Timestamp verified

✓ Location verified

✓ Community consensus

✓ Organization endorsement

✓ AI integrity scan

Users must understand why confidence exists.

---

# Principle 4

## Transparency over Authority

OpenWitness values transparent processes over authoritative decisions.

Verification history should always remain visible.

Evidence should never silently change.

Every modification should leave an audit trail.

---

# Principle 5

## Preserve Original Evidence

Original uploads are never overwritten.

Every transformation creates a new version.

Every edit is recorded.

Every version remains accessible.

---

# Principle 6

## Every Claim Needs Evidence

Claims should never exist alone.

↓

Evidence

Verification

↓

Confidence

↓

History

# Principle 8

## AI Assists Humans

AI never decides truth.

AI only assists reviewers by:

Metadata extraction

OCR

Speech transcription

Landmark detection

Tampering detection

Duplicate detection

Summarization

Humans remain responsible.

---

# Principle 9

## Reputation is Earned

Nobody receives trust automatically.

Trust is earned through:

Identity verification

Accurate contributions

Successful reviews

Community endorsement

Long-term participation

Transparency

---

# Principle 10

## Context Matters

Evidence without context is dangerous.

Every evidence page should answer:

Who?

Where?

When?

How?

Why?

Source?

Verification?

Related evidence?

---

# Principle 11

## Preserve History

Evidence should remain accessible even after events end.

OpenWitness is an archive.

History should remain searchable.

---

# Principle 12

## Open by Default

Technology

Documentation

Architecture

APIs

Specifications

Roadmaps

---

# Principle 13

## Privacy by Design

Whistleblowers

Personal information

# Principle 14

## Safety Before Virality

OpenWitness does not optimize for engagement.

# Principle 15

## Global but Neutral
